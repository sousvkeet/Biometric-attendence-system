For a good scan – the device compares all three scans:
  • Use the same finger in the same position each time, pressed flat so it covers the sensor.
  • Hold still until the device beeps, then lift the finger completely before the next scan.
  • Dry or sweaty finger? Wipe it (or breathe on a very dry finger) and keep the sensor glass clean.
